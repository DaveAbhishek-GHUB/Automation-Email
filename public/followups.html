How Sequences Work
Each sequence has a Launch Email (sent on Day 0) and a Follow-up Email that fires at your chosen time each day. The scheduler checks every minute — it sends follow-ups at exactly the time you set. If the app was off when the time passed, it catches up and sends immediately on next start. All steps are fully automatic after launch.
